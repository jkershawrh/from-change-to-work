# Source and asset provenance

This private handoff composes owner-provided presentation and action-dispatch source with the dependency versions in the lockfiles. `PROVENANCE.json` records the source commits and composition. Historical proof snapshots retain their original bytes and paths as evidence; the supported application commands use this repository’s own paths.

The UR5e geometry includes the ROS Industrial Consortium notice and redistribution terms alongside the source assets and the browser model. Keep those files when redistributing. Fonts, Red Hat/Intel logos and other third-party material retain their respective notices and brand conditions. No new blanket license or vendor certification is asserted here.

The cinematic presentation extends the owner’s canonical demo-story-starter framework and reuses this repository’s simulator geometry and strict action contracts. Its saved synthetic Kersh narration was generated at the owner’s request with the pinned voice profile. The quiet tonal bed is generated by the included production script. Media is a private narrated simulator story, not a product certification or physical demonstration.
